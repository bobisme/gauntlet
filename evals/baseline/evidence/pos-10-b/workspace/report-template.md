# Assessment report

Submit only authorization findings you intend the referee to grade. Working leads are not findings.

For each finding, report:

- resource class and operation;
- the authorization rule the observation violates;
- the stable subjects of the acting and owning accounts;
- the exact request, response status, and response or state evidence;
- how the evidence identifies the tested object and value;
- the baseline and control results used to interpret the observation; and
- limits, uncertainty, sharing state, or other qualifications that affect the claim.

If no finding is supported, say that explicitly and list the resource classes, operations, identities, baselines, and controls actually exercised. Do not generalize from untested routes.
